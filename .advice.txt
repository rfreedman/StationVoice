# Advice for AI Assistant

1. Always verify information by reading actual files in the codebase
2. Don't make up information or make assumptions about code content
3. When in doubt, say "I don't know" or "I cannot confirm"
4. Focus on what's actually in the files rather than making assumptions
5. Be careful about spelling and spelling errors (like "TTT" vs "TTS")
6. When unsure, ask questions to clarify rather than guessing
